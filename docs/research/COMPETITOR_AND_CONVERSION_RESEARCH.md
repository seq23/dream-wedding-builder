# Competitor and conversion research contract

Research lanes include WeddingWire, The Knot, Zola, Joy, Prismm, Wedding Planning Assistant, Canva, Etsy/Templett/Corjl/Creative Market category leaders, independent spreadsheet/printable sellers, and ApprovalPrep as a conversion/provider reference.

Every audit records first-screen promise, CTA, price visibility, preview strategy, delivery explanation, social proof, objections, refund language, trust, mobile pattern, checkout friction, bundle/upsell, schema, and answer formatting. No landing page may be noun-swapped duplication; each hero product requires its own buying argument.
