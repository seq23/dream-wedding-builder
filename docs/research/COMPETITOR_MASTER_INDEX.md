# Competitor Master Index

| Competitor | Category | Strength | Opportunity for this repo |
|---|---|---|---|
| WeddingWire | Broad free platform | Integrated checklist, budget, guest and seating tools | Sell deeper final handoffs and downloadable operational packages |
| The Knot | Broad marketplace/platform | Strong brand, vendors, budget and checklist | Avoid broad planning battle; own focused paid execution queries |
| Zola | Registry/planning ecosystem | RSVP-linked guest tools and exports | Differentiate on no-registry, file-first execution outcomes |
| Prismm | Professional event visualization | Venue floor plans and pro workflows | Serve couples who need a simpler, lower-cost handoff system |
| Joy | Free wedding site and guest management | Strong free communication layer | Do not compete on websites; convert on paid execution artifacts |
| Canva | Template/design ecosystem | Flexible visual templates | Differentiate with wedding logic, formulas, reconciliation and instructions |
| Etsy/Templett/Corjl sellers | Paid downloads | Visual choice, low price, immediate delivery | Win on operational depth, versioning, support, and connected product family |
