# Hero Tool Conversion Audit

## Research conclusion
Large wedding platforms compete by offering broad free planning ecosystems. The paid products in this repo therefore must sell final-mile execution rather than generic access to a blank template.

## Patterns observed
- WeddingWire: broad free planning stack, guest-list integration, floor-plan seating, budget estimates, and checklist scheduling.
- Zola: account-based planning, RSVP-linked seating, Excel export, checklist and registry ecosystem.
- Prismm/AllSeated: professional-grade floor plans, venue layouts, Excel import, and event visualization.
- The Knot: extensive planning suite, budgeter, checklist, RSVP management, vendor marketplace.
- Marketplace downloads: visual previews, immediate delivery, low prices, but inconsistent depth, support, versioning, and operational guidance.

## Conversion decisions
1. Price and one-time-purchase terms are visible above the fold.
2. Every product page states the exact finished outcome, not just features.
3. Every product has a distinct audience, process, objection set, and handoff.
4. The product family explicitly contrasts broad free planning with paid execution depth.
5. Immediate protected delivery and support contact are stated before checkout.
6. Generic testimonials are not fabricated; proof is limited to demonstrable product contents and verified customer evidence when available.
7. Product previews and sample pages are required release assets.
8. All pages carry plain-English boundaries and product-specific verification duties.
