# Paid Landing Page Authority Destination Contract

The four paid product landing pages are the primary destinations for Query Intelligence, Atlas ownership, fanout, internal linking, SEO, AEO, GEO, LLM corpora, and conversion.

## Allocation
- Paid product domains: 85–90% of content-factory attention.
- Free Builder: 10–15%, limited to parent-brand onboarding and product recommendation.

## Routing priority
1. Supporting authority page to its paid landing page.
2. Supporting authority page to related paid-domain content.
3. Paid landing page to checkout.
4. Paid landing page to the complete Suite.
5. Free Builder to the matching paid product.

## Hard failures
- A paid-product query is assigned to the free Builder.
- A generated route has no paid-product owner.
- A commercial CTA omits price.
- A supporting page outranks the flagship in internal-link priority by configuration.
- Cross-domain duplication creates competing canonical ownership.
