# Paid Product Preview Distribution Packet

## Purpose

Promote the four product pages by showing prospects what the paid files actually contain before checkout. This replaces the discarded free-template acquisition layer.

## Hard boundary

- Do not distribute editable CSV, XLSX, or full-resolution paid PDF files.
- Do not describe preview images as free templates or free downloads.
- Every public preview must remain flattened, watermarked, and tied to a canonical paid release source.
- Link to the product page or its `#look-inside` section, not directly to private release files.
- A submitted link is not a backlink. Record a backlink only after a live page and evidence exist.

Use only where a roundup covers paid planning tools, wedding spreadsheet products, printable planning systems, or transparent product comparisons. Do not pitch these pages as free printable resources.

## Community response structure

1. Answer the planning question completely in the post.
2. Disclose ownership before linking.
3. Explain exactly which paid-file view is relevant.
4. Link only when community rules permit commercial resources.
5. Never ask for votes, coordinate engagement, or repost the same answer across communities.
6. Track submitted, approved, live, nofollow, removed, and rejected separately.
